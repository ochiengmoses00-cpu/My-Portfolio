About us

An engineering-led climate & energy practice.

Terraverde Impact is a Nairobi-based solar PV engineering and climate advisory firm. We help distributors, operators, governments and investors design and deliver renewable energy systems and climate-aligned initiatives that perform on site — not just on paper.

Our work blends mechanical and energy engineering, environmental science and project finance to produce defensible designs, auditable technical documentation and credible impact reporting. Every project we touch is shaped by field evidence, engineering discipline and a bias toward outcomes that stand up to scrutiny.
