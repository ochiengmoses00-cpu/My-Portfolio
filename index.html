About us

A carbon project developer that engineers for integrity.

Verst Carbon is a Nairobi-based carbon project developer and climate advisory firm working at the intersection of carbon finance, clean energy and climate science. We design, document and deliver high-integrity carbon projects for distributors, operators, governments and investors across Sub-Saharan Africa.

Our team blends mechanical and energy engineering, environmental science and policy to produce defensible baselines, auditable MRV and submission-ready documentation. Every project we touch is shaped by field evidence, methodology discipline and a bias toward credits that stand up to scrutiny.

Sectors

Where we work.

Clean Cooking

Improved cookstoves, EPCs, induction, LPG, biogas, bioethanol and pellet systems — household and institutional, under GS MECD and RECH.

Animal Waste Management

Household and institutional biodigesters with biogas thermal use under GS AWMS v2.0 — methane avoidance, leakage, digestate accounting.

Renewable Energy

Solar PV system design, mini-grids, geothermal and biomass energy — techno-economic modelling, PVsyst and HOMER Pro simulation.

E-Mobility & Transport

Electric two and three wheeler programmes, endline and operator surveys, charging and battery practice, MRV design for mobility credits.
